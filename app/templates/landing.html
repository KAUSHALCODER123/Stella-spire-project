Screening you can argue with

Most tools hand you a score and ask you to trust it. This one shows its working, and tells you when it could not.

Every quote is checked

Each claim is located in the source CV. Anything the tool cannot find is stamped UNVERIFIED rather than quietly presented as fact.

Dates are computed, not guessed

Experience, tenure and employment gaps come from arithmetic on the CV's own dates, so they are right every time and marked as computed.

Padding gets caught

Skills demonstrated in real work are separated from skills merely listed — the fastest signal that a CV has been inflated.

How a match is made

Four steps. Only the last one costs anything, which is why screening stays affordable at scale.

Describe the role

A structured form, so the brief needs no parsing at all.

Rule out the impossible

Pay band, notice period and location are compared as declared facts. Free.

Screen for overlap

Every remaining pair is scored on what the CV actually evidences. Also free.

Assess what survives

Only promising pairs get a full read, with a quote behind every judgement.
